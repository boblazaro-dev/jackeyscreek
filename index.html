Developer commits to

• Donate land in fee simple: about 20-acre park site plus the Spine Road connector site
• Build the Spine Road (two lanes, 15’ landscaped median) from US 17 to NC 133
• Build a roundabout at Spine Road and Jackey’s Crossing, with right-of-way for a second lane
• Best efforts on the Jackey’s Crossing extension toward the pump station
• Design and pay for all infrastructure; dedicate water and sewer lines to the Town and H2GO
• Indemnify and defend the Town against third-party legal challenges

Town commits to

• Offer water and sewer service through assets jointly owned with H2GO, subject to availability
• Grant an easement for the Spine Road
• Best efforts to build the Jackey’s Crossing connection over Jackeys Creek to Westgate, subject to funding, permits, and approvals
• Review permits in a reasonable timeframe and cooperate on state and federal permits
• Allow listed design concessions: wood bridges, HDPE pipe, model homes, signage

Key terms and protections

• Vested rights: rules in force on the Effective Date apply for the full term; no later ordinances or moratoria without developer consent, except as required by law
• Oversight: Town Manager reviews compliance every 12 months
• Default: reasonable cure period, never under 90 days; termination appealable to the Board of Adjustment
• No construction mandate: the developer is not obligated to build the Project, but completion is a precondition of the Town’s water and sewer obligations
• Fiscal protection: no pledge of the Town’s faith and credit
